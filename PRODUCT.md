# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing scaffold: Vite + React 19 + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4 (Vite plugin), framer-motion (spring transitions preferred), react-router for a separate Menu page. Requested by the user.

## Users
- Pavia locals choosing where to have lunch or a dinner out (couples, families, colleagues near the Comune and San Michele).
- Visitors and tourists in Pavia, often on a phone, looking for an authentic Lombard/Pavese trattoria. The site is bilingual IT/EN for them (confirmed).
Job: decide quickly whether this is the place, check the menu and prices, check that it is open, and book.

## Product Purpose
Website of Trattoria Ressi, a small family-run historic trattoria in central Pavia. Success = more bookings by phone/WhatsApp and visitors trusting the place before they arrive. It must be clearly better than the current WordPress site (trattoriaressi.com).

## Positioning
A Pavese trattoria with over 80 years of tradition, in a single 40-seat room under an exposed red-brick barrel vault on via Adeodato Ressi, along the old walls. Seasonal Lombard cooking built on Pavia rice (Carnaroli Cascina Alberona), house-made pasta, bread, focaccia and desserts (torta Paradiso, turta d'armandul from Varzi), and a list of 100+ wines mostly from the Oltrepò Pavese.

## Operating Context

- Hours (confirmed, from the official Contatti page): lunch 12:30-14:00, dinner 19:30-21:30; closed Sunday evening and all day Wednesday.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Two routes: Home and Menu (`/menu`).
- Tasting menu "Ticinum" €50 per person, whole table, not combinable.
- Vegetarian and gluten-free options are listed by directories.
- Undecided / not available: online booking engine, Google rating figure (not retrievable), exact founding year (sources conflict: "over 80 years" vs "the 50s"; use the restaurant's own "oltre 80 anni").

## Brand Commitments
- Name "Trattoria Ressi". Existing logo/sign: `public/images/insegna.png`, `public/images/insegna-trasparente.png`.
- Voice: warm, plain, Italian family trattoria. No invented superlatives.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real photos from the official site in `public/images/` (room, vault, entrance, street, dishes: risotto, ravioli, polpo, black cod, agnello, Carthusia amaro).
- TripAdvisor: 4.2/5, ~340 reviews, top 30 of 400+ restaurants in Pavia; Lonely Planet recommendation noted on the listing.
- Review themes (paraphrase only, no fabricated quotes): risotto praised as the best in Pavia, house bread and desserts, brick-vaulted room, courteous staff.
- Absent: Google rating, verbatim review texts with author names. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles
1. Booking is one tap away on every screen.
2. Show the room and the plates; the photos are the proof.
3. Tell the truth: seasonal menu, indicative prices, real hours.
4. Pavia first: rice, Oltrepò wine, the walls, San Michele.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Bilingual IT/EN. Older local audience: readable type sizes, strong contrast, reduced-motion support.
